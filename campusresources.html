UT Austin Freshman Resources

Navigating your first year on the Forty Acres can feel overwhelming, but having the right resources can make the transition much easier. Here are some important UT Austin resources every freshman should know.

The Absolute Essentials

Texas One Stop

Your main hub for registration, transcripts, financial aid, tuition payments, and important student records.

Visit Texas One Stop

Student Technology Hub

Use this resource to manage your UT EID, set up Duo two-factor authentication, and access important technology services.

Visit Technology Hub

Canvas & Microsoft 365

Canvas is where professors post assignments, grades, announcements, and course materials. Students also receive Microsoft 365 for free.

Open Canvas

Academic Support

Sanger Learning Center (SLC)

Provides tutoring, study groups, and review sessions to help students succeed in challenging courses.

Get Academic Support

University Writing Center

Get help with essays, research papers, resumes, and improving your writing skills.

Visit Writing Center

Vick Advising Excellence Center

Helpful for students exploring majors, considering a change, or looking for academic and career guidance.

Visit Vick Center

Health, Wellness, and Safety

University Health Services (UHS)

Provides medical care, vaccinations, appointments, and health support for UT students.

Visit Health Services

Counseling and Mental Health Center

Offers counseling, group support, and mental health resources to help students manage stress and challenges.

Visit CMHC

Behavior Concerns Advice Line (BCAL)

A 24/7 resource for concerns about yourself, classmates, roommates, or other students.

Phone: 512-232-5050

Campus Life & Transportation

Interactive UT Campus Map

Use the campus map to find buildings, classrooms, printers, study locations, and important campus services.

Open UT Map

Capital Metro (CapMetro)

UT students can ride Austin buses for free using their UT student ID card.

Visit CapMetro

Recreational Sports

Access Gregory Gym, fitness classes, intramural sports, and recreation facilities.

Explore Recreation

Get Involved

Student Organizations

Joining organizations is a great way to meet people, build connections, and become part of the UT community.
