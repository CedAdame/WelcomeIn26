Starting at UT Austin can bring a lot of questions. Here are answers to some common questions freshmen have when beginning their journey on the Forty Acres.

How do I get my UT EID?

Your UT EID is your official university account. You can create or manage it through the UT Technology Hub. You will use it for Canvas, registration, email, and many other UT services.

Where can I find my class assignments?

Most professors use Canvas to post assignments, grades, announcements, and course materials. Check Canvas regularly because every class may be organized differently.

How do I register for classes?

Class registration is completed through Texas One Stop. Check your registration times, meet with your advisor when needed, and register as early as possible because classes can fill quickly.

What should I do if I am struggling in a class?

Do not wait until the end of the semester. UT offers tutoring, study groups, and academic support through resources like the Sanger Learning Center and the University Writing Center.

Where can I get help choosing a major?

If you are unsure about your major or want to explore different options, the Vick Advising Excellence Center helps students discover academic and career paths.

How do I get my student ID card?

Your UT ID card gives you access to campus services, buildings, and transportation benefits. Visit the Texas One Stop or Student Services resources for information about getting your card.

Is healthcare available on campus?

Yes. University Health Services provides medical care, vaccinations, appointments, and health support for UT students.

What if I feel overwhelmed or stressed?

College can be challenging. The Counseling and Mental Health Center provides support, counseling services, and resources to help students manage stress and personal challenges.

How do I get involved on campus?

Joining student organizations is a great way to meet people and build your UT community. Explore clubs, events, leadership opportunities, and campus activities.

Where can I ask for more help?

If you have questions or need guidance, use our contact page to send a message and get connected with support.
